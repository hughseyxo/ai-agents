# New wilt appearing — 28-day watering too long

A direct photo comparison between 2026-07-12 (top-down, foliage upright and turgid) and 2026-07-15 (side-on) shows the Lantana has begun to wilt: tip-leaf clusters and lower leaves are now drooping and limp, and several lower leaves are yellowing/browning and folding near the soil. Importantly this appears only **3 days after watering**, which points away from the earlier "hydration is fine, the problem is purely structural" reading.

The likely cause is the watering schedule. The plant sits in a **terracotta pot outdoors in full summer sun** — a combination that dries out very fast, often within 2–4 days. The current 28–30 day baseline is wildly out of step with those conditions, so the plant is probably repeatedly drying out between waterings, producing the heat/moisture stress now visible.

Action: feel the soil 3–5cm down now and water thoroughly if dry (almost certainly the case), and drop the watering frequency to roughly every 4 days, adjusting by soil feel rather than a fixed calendar. Do not let the pot dry out fully between waterings during the summer heat.

The separate, long-running issues — leggy open framework with foliage only at the branch tips, and bloom stalled at unopened green bud spikes — are unchanged. The corrective ~1/3 tip prune above leaf nodes, deadheading, and a potassium feed still appear not to have been carried out and should follow once watering is corrected.
