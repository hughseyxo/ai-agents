# Canopy rebuilding after summer plateau

After several weeks of a sparse, leggy plateau (documented in the 07-30 and 08-06 photos), this fuchsia is now showing a genuine rebound. The mid-section of the grow-bag has filled in with noticeably denser, greener, turgid foliage, and flowering has continued with several open magenta pendant blooms and fresh buds across the mid and lower trailing stems.

This appears to be a direct result of the tip-pinching and canopy-rebuild advice given in the August assessments — the branching is starting to fill gaps rather than the plant simply extending a few leggy stems. It marks the transition from 'partial recovery that plateaued' to active re-densification.

The one persistent caveat is the upper crown near the bag opening, which still carries brown-crisped, mottled leaves. This is the driest zone of the vertical bag and dries top-first, so the fix remains mechanical (pinch out dead tissue) and watering-method based (pour slowly at the top so the crown is actually wetted) rather than a change to the overall watering cadence.
