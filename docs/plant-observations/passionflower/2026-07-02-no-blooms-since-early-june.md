# No blooms since early June

## Observation

User reported no blooms for an extended period (2026-07-02). Last confirmed flower buds were in the **2026-06-04 photo assessment**; the **2026-06-18 assessment** already noted no flowers or buds visible despite excellent vegetative health.

Plant is producing very dense, vigorous foliage with active tendrils — consistent strong-health trend — but no flowering.

## Likely Causes

1. **Vegetative dominance** — heavy foliage growth suggests energy may be going to leaves rather than flowers; possible high-nitrogen conditions.
2. **Inter-flush pause** — Passiflora blooms in waves; a 3–6 week vegetative phase between flushes is normal.
3. **Bud drop from the June watering stress** — catch-up watering on 2026-06-16 (5 days overdue) may have caused bud drop before buds matured.
4. **Pot size** — if recently repotted into a larger container, plant may prioritise root/foliage growth before returning to bloom.

## Actions

- Switch to a high-K/P feed (e.g. tomato fertiliser) every 1–2 weeks through July; avoid high-nitrogen general fertiliser.
- Maintain consistent 4-day watering baseline — avoid long gaps.
- Do not prune — flower buds form on new growth.
- Monitor for next flush of buds; if none by end of July, reassess feeding and pot situation.
