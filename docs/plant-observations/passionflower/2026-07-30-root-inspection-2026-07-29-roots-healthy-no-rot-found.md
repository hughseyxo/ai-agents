# Root inspection 2026-07-29 — roots healthy, no rot found

## Root inspection finding — 2026-07-29

On unpotting, roots were **healthy** — firm and white, no mushy or brown tissue, no sour smell.

**Revised diagnosis:** Not true root rot (fungal). Likely **soil anaerobia** — prolonged waterlogging deprived roots of oxygen, impairing uptake and causing wilt/dieback despite moist substrate. Symptoms mirror root rot but without tissue death.

**Implication:** Recovery prognosis is better than suspected. No rotten tissue to excise; the root system is intact and functional. The plant shed foliage as a transpiration-reduction response it couldn't reverse while soil was waterlogged.

## Current priority
- Fresh free-draining medium is the fix — roots now have oxygen again
- **Do not water** until top 3–4 cm confirmed dry; this is critical to not repeat the waterlogging
- No fungicide needed
- Expect new growth from the green framework in 1–2 weeks as roots re-establish in the new medium
