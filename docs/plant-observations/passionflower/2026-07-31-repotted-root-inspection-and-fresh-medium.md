# Repotted — root inspection and fresh medium

## Repotting — 2026-07-28

Repotted on 2026-07-28 (3 days ago as of 2026-07-31), actioning the standing unpot-and-inspect advice that had been flagged since 07-28.

**What was done:**
- Unpotted the plant and inspected the root ball
- Repotted into fresh free-draining medium in a clean pot

**Context:**
- Plant had shown five consecutive frames of decline (07-27→07-31) on moist soil, strongly indicating root rot / impaired uptake
- A watering on 07-29 (against standing withhold advice) had failed to halt deterioration, further confirming root function was the issue, not thirst
- The main climbing framework (firm, upright, green stems) remained alive throughout — the plant was not dead

**Current status post-repot (07-31):**
- Still showing near-bare stems and limp low-centre foliage — too early to see recovery
- No further watering until top 3-4cm is confirmed bone dry
- Hard-pruning of dead leaves and dried tendrils recommended if not already done
- Move to afternoon shade to reduce transpiration demand during recovery

**Watch for:**
- New growth from green stem nodes = roots recovering
- Continued or worsening dieback after 7-10 days = root damage may have been too extensive
