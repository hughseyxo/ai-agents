# Passionflower recovery stalling — no reshoots, green receding

The passionflower's post-strip-back recovery has moved from 'paused' to 'stalling'. As of 2026-08-10, roughly a week after the Aug 3 cut-back to a bare framework, there are still **no reshoots anywhere**, and the band of surviving green tissue has been visibly retreating over the last several photos (Aug 5 → Aug 6 → Aug 9 → Aug 10). Green now survives only in short segments near the upper green trellis clips and a small part of the central vertical stem; almost everything else has dried to straw-tan.

This matters because passionflower regrows vigorously from live green stems, so that shrinking remnant of green is the plant's only recovery tissue. The earlier plan was to verify stem viability around Aug 14-16, but the continued browning warrants pulling that check forward: a **scratch test now** (thumbnail scrape to look for moist green cambium on the central and upper stems) is the fastest way to know whether this is still a live, recoverable plant or a failing one.

Watering is not the limiting factor right now — the plant was watered Aug 9 and, being leafless, transpires almost nothing, so the root ball stays moist for days. The priority is to hold water until the soil dries, keep the framework unpruned except for stems confirmed dead by the scratch test, maintain temporary midday shade, and re-photo in about 4-5 days. If green cambium is still present, recovery may yet come; if the main stems scratch dry brown, the plant should be considered lost.
